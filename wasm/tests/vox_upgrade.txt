# The upgrade to a Voxelmon (deck.rs + vox.rs showcase, Kaigen vox_showcase): a 6-energy card at its last card stage
# upgraded in the deck screen bursts, the screen opens onto the town and the 3D beast rises out of two glowing rings
# between you and the camera, spinning and growing; "Let's go!" closes the moment and it walks with you. "Walk with
# me" on another Voxelmon swaps the one that follows you.

screen play
